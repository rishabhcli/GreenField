01The operating loop

A company behaves like a loop, not a launch.

Yellofield runs the full business cycle continuously — observe, score, source, build, launch, measure, re-plan. Results feed back to the CEO agent, and the next iteration starts smarter than the last.

The circuit

Twenty steps. One heartbeat.

Each step is executed by the organization — managers delegate, specialists do the work, and every result lands in the audit trail before the next step begins.

20 steps, one closed circuit,
executed by the organization
  1. 01

    Observe

    Mine real public conversations — reviews, forums, communities — for demand signals.

  2. 02

    Extract

    Pull complaints, workarounds, failure modes, and willingness-to-pay language into evidence packets.

  3. 03

    Cluster

    Deduplicate and group signals into candidate pain points with source traceability.

  4. 04

    Score

    Severity, frequency, margin, sourcing feasibility, legal risk — weighted, never hidden.

  5. 05

    Ask experts

    Terac human reviewers judge what machine research can't. Overrides are recorded with rationale.

  6. 06

    Select

    The CEO commits to an opportunity inside capital-allocation policy.

  7. 07

    Source

    Find suppliers able to produce or private-label. Verify capability, MOQ, customization.

  8. 08

    Cost it

    Full landed cost — freight, duties, QC, fulfillment — down to contribution margin.

  9. 09

    Negotiate

    RFQs, samples, and terms compared side by side. Assumptions stay separate from quoted values.

  10. 10

    Brand

    Name, identity, packaging, positioning — checked against trademark and domain signals.

  11. 11

    Build

    Storefront generated through Lovable, checkout wired to Stripe, deployed on Render.

  12. 12

    Create

    Ad concepts and page copy generated as testable variants, not single shots.

  13. 13

    Review

    High-impact creative passes human expert review before a dollar is spent.

  14. 14

    Launch

    Controlled experiments with hypotheses, budgets, stop conditions, and KPI targets.

  15. 15

    Measure

    CTR, CAC, conversion, contribution margin, refund rate — profit, not vanity metrics.

  16. 16

    Reallocate

    Kill weak variants, scale winners within spend caps, generate the next hypotheses.

  17. 17

    QA

    Replay autonomously walks the live site. Critical failures veto the release.

  18. 18

    Report

    Operational results return to the CEO as structured reports, not chat transcripts.

  19. 19

    Re-plan

    Strategy updates from evidence. Kill, continue, or pivot — decided, not drifted.

  20. 20

    Repeat

    The company improves recursively. The loop is the organization's heartbeat.
